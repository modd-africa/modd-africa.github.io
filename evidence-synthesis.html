Evidence Synthesis

The Evidence Synthesis Unit collates, analyses and communicates the evidence behind MoDD Africa’s models and advice. The tooling is open and reproducible.

The unit

From data to decisions

The Evidence Synthesis Unit runs a repeatable pipeline from raw data to decision-ready outputs: acquiring and reviewing existing data, re-formatting it for modelling, building shared databases and visual tools (such as MosquitoDB and interactive Shiny apps), then analysing, validating and packaging results for National Malaria Programmes and partners — and updating them as new data and feedback arrive.

The modelling framework spans species composition (single- and multi-species), spatial-temporal methods (e.g. GAMs and Bayesian hierarchical models) and explicit seasonality, driven by fine-resolution climate and environmental data and structured from national down to sub-national levels — so outputs can inform practical decisions such as timing IRS and LLIN campaigns to align with peak transmission.

Open tooling

The moddafricaes package

moddafricaes — MoDD Africa Evidence Synthesis — is the unit’s R package for collating, analysing and communicating evidence. Its first end-to-end pipeline turns ERA5-Land climate reanalysis into analysis-ready data: derive a study-area bounding box from a shapefile, download hourly NetCDFs from the Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS), and transform them into Parquet (hourly or monthly) or GeoTIFF (monthly minimum / maximum temperature).

The source lives on GitHub: github.com/modd-africa/moddafricaes.

Install

# pak (recommended — resolves system dependencies too)
# install.packages("pak")
pak::pak("modd-africa/moddafricaes")

# or with remotes / devtools
remotes::install_github("modd-africa/moddafricaes")

You will also need a Copernicus CDS account and an API key exported as CDS_API_KEY (see https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/).

The pipeline

All bounding boxes use Copernicus CDS order c(N, W, S, E) — not sf::st_bbox()’s (xmin, ymin, xmax, ymax).
Function Stage Output
get_bounding_box() Setup c(N, W, S, E) numeric vector (CDS order)
extract_era5land() Download One NetCDF per (year, month)
transform_era5land() Transform One Parquet per year (hourly)
transform_era5land_monthly() Transform One Parquet (monthly means)
transform_era5land_minmax() Transform GeoTIFF stacks of monthly 2 m-temperature min / max

End to end

library(moddafricaes)

# 1. Derive a bounding box from a shapefile (zipped shapefiles work via /vsizip/)
bbox <- get_bounding_box(
  "/vsizip/utilities/province26.zip/provinces26/Province26.shp"
)

# 2. Download hourly ERA5-Land NetCDFs for the bbox (needs CDS_API_KEY)
extract_era5land(
  bbox       = bbox,
  years      = 2020:2021,
  output_dir = "data/era5land/nc"
)

# 3. Transform hourly NetCDFs into one analysis-ready Parquet per year
transform_era5land(
  input_dir  = "data/era5land/nc",
  output_dir = "data/era5land/parquet",
  dem_tif    = "RDC_Elevation_Complete.tif",
  years      = 2020:2021
)

The hourly transform de-accumulates precipitation and downward shortwave radiation per day, derives wind speed, relative humidity, vapour-pressure deficit and Celsius temperature, and joins DEM-derived elevation onto the ERA5-Land 0.1° grid. Every write step is idempotent: an existing readable output short-circuits the work, while a corrupt one is deleted and regenerated, so re-runs resume cleanly.

Known caveat. Forcing the de-accumulated daily totals to zero at hour 0 is exact only where 00 UTC falls during the night (as in central Africa). For areas where 00 UTC is daylight, real shortwave flux would be silently zeroed at the day boundary — revisit before applying the pipeline elsewhere.
